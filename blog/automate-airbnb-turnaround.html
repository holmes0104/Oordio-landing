How to Automate Your Airbnb Turnaround Cleaning

Practical tips for hosts

If you're an Airbnb host, you know the drill: guest checks out at 11am, new guest arrives at 3pm, and somewhere in between you need a spotless property. The problem? Coordinating cleaners by text, chasing confirmations, and hoping nothing falls through the cracks.

The Manual Trap

Many hosts spend hours every week on cleaning coordination—texting primary cleaners, scrambling for backups when they're unavailable, and manually tracking who's doing what. At 10–15 minutes per booking (finding someone, confirming, following up), a single property with 12 turnarounds a month eats 2–3 hours. Add a second or third property, and you're drowning in spreadsheets and group chats.

Worse, manual coordination is fragile. A missed text, a cleaner who doesn't reply in time, or a last-minute cancellation can leave you scrambling hours before the next guest arrives. One delayed check-in can cost you a 5-star review and future bookings.

What Automation Actually Does

Modern tools like Oordio connect to your property management system (Airbnb, VRBO, iCal feeds) and automatically detect when a booking is confirmed. A cleaning job is created immediately, with checkout time driving the timeline. The system then offers the job to your primary cleaner via push notification—no text from you required.

If your primary declines or doesn't respond within the acceptance window, Oordio escalates to your backup cleaner. If both are unavailable and marketplace is enabled, it creates a marketplace posting for vetted cleaners to bid on. You get a dashboard view of everything: Pending → Offered → Accepted → In Progress → Completed. No spreadsheets, no chasing.

The key is that automation handles the repetitive work—detecting checkouts, creating jobs, offering to cleaners, sending reminders, escalating when needed. You're notified only when human intervention is required (e.g. all options exhausted).

Getting Started

  1. Define your primary and backup cleaners — Assign them per property in Oordio so the system knows who to contact first. Your primary gets the offer; if they can't make it, your backup gets it automatically.
  2. Connect your calendar — Add your Airbnb or VRBO iCal URL, or use the Airbnb webhook if available. Oordio syncs bookings and creates jobs as soon as they're confirmed.
  3. Set acceptance windows — Oordio uses dynamic windows based on urgency (e.g. 48 hours when checkout is far away, 4 hours when it's urgent). You can override per property if needed.
  4. Use digital checklists — Standardize what "clean" means so every property meets guest expectations. Cleaners see the checklist in the app and check off items as they complete them.

The Result

Automation isn't about replacing your cleaners—it's about replacing the manual back-and-forth. You keep your trusted team; the system handles the logistics. That frees you to focus on guest experience, pricing, and growth instead of texting "Can you do Tuesday at 11?" for the hundredth time.
