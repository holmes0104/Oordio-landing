What Oordio Can Do: The Complete Feature Guide

Everything you need to know about Oordio's capabilities for hosts and cleaners

Oordio is a landlord–cleaner coordination platform built specifically for short-term rental operators. It connects your bookings, cleaners, payments, and schedules into one automated system—so you can stop chasing people by text and start running your rentals with confidence. Here's a detailed breakdown of everything Oordio can do.

1. Booking Ingestion & Sync

Oordio automatically detects when bookings are confirmed and creates cleaning jobs without manual data entry.

  • Airbnb webhook integration — When a booking is confirmed on Airbnb, Oordio ingests it via webhook and creates a job immediately. Checkout time (end_at) drives the assignment timeline.
  • iCal feed sync — For Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and Agoda, you can add your property's iCal URL. Oordio syncs the feed periodically, detects new checkouts, and creates jobs. Supports multiple platforms per property.
  • Manual import — For direct bookings or other sources, you can add bookings manually. Oordio still creates jobs and runs the full assignment flow.

Jobs are created as soon as a booking reaches CONFIRMED status and checkout time is available. No spreadsheets, no copy-paste.

2. Relationship-First Assignment Ladder

Oordio assigns cleaners in a strict priority order—your people first, marketplace as fallback.

  1. Primary cleaner — Your main cleaner per property. Oordio offers the job to them first with a time-limited acceptance window.
  2. Backup cleaner — If primary declines or doesn't respond in time, the job is offered to your backup. Same flow, shorter window.
  3. Marketplace — If both are unavailable and marketplace is enabled, Oordio creates a marketplace posting. Cleaners can browse and bid; you review and accept.
  4. Unassigned — If all options are exhausted, Oordio notifies you once so you can intervene manually.

Each step is automated. You don't have to text anyone or move jobs between spreadsheets.

3. Dynamic Acceptance Windows

Oordio adjusts how long cleaners have to accept based on urgency. Every offer must expire at least 48 hours before checkout so you always have time to escalate.

Stage When checkout > 48h away Urgent (≤48h) Same-day
Primary48 hours4 hours60 min
Backup24 hours2 hours30 min
Marketplace12 hours60 min20 min

Per-property overrides let you tune these windows. The minimum is always 15 minutes—cleaners always get time to respond.

4. Smart Reminders (No Spam)

Oordio sends push notifications to cleaners at strategic points so they don't miss offers:

  • 1 notification when the offer is sent
  • Up to 3 reminders spaced across the window: 50%, 75%, and 90% elapsed (skipped for very short windows)
  • Max 4 notifications per job per cleaner—no spam
  • Silent expiration — no "offer expired" ping; Oordio just moves to the next step

5. Per-Booking Cleaning Checklists

Standardize quality across your portfolio with digital checklists.

  • Define checklist templates per property (e.g. "Standard Turnaround", "Deep Clean")
  • Cleaners see the checklist in the app when they accept a job
  • Check off items as they complete them
  • Photo verification for key items (optional)

Guests get consistent quality; you get peace of mind without micromanaging.

6. Interval Cleanings

For longer stays, Oordio can schedule recurring cleanings between bookings. Enable interval cleanings per property and set the frequency (e.g. every 7 days). Oordio creates jobs automatically so the property stays fresh even during multi-week stays.

7. Booking Time Changes

When checkout time changes by 30 minutes or more, Oordio:

  1. Notifies the assigned cleaner to reconfirm (60-minute window)
  2. If they decline, restarts the flow at backup or marketplace
  3. If there's a pending offer, cancels it and restarts with the new time

No stale assignments. No confusion about when to show up.

8. Payments & Payouts (Stripe)

Oordio integrates with Stripe for end-to-end payment handling:

  • Auto capture — When a job is marked complete, Oordio can automatically capture the payment from the guest/host
  • Auto payouts — Funds are transferred to the cleaner's connected Stripe account after job completion
  • Ledger & tracking — See who's been paid, what's pending, and export for accounting

Cleaners get paid on time without invoicing. Hosts avoid manual payment runs.

9. Unified Calendar & Dashboard

One view for your entire operation:

  • Bookings from all sources (Airbnb, VRBO, iCal, manual) in one calendar
  • Cleaning status alongside occupancy: Pending → Offered → Accepted → In Progress → Completed
  • Portfolio dashboards with hygiene metrics, assignment funnel (primary/backup/marketplace), and occupancy insights

10. Role-Based Access

Oordio has two roles with strict data scoping:

  • Landlord — Full CRUD on their properties, bookings, jobs. Can invite cleaners, manage subscriptions, view analytics.
  • Cleaner — Sees only assigned properties, bookings, and jobs. No access to other hosts' data or financials.

Row-level security (RLS) enforces this in the database. Cleaners never see your full portfolio.

11. Web & Mobile Apps

Oordio runs on:

  • Web — app.oordio.com. Use it from any browser.
  • iOS & Android — Native apps via Expo. Push notifications, GPS check-ins, offline-friendly where possible.

Same account, same data. Hosts can monitor from a laptop; cleaners can accept jobs and manage routes from their phone.

12. Event Logging & Audit Trail

Every assignment event is logged: OFFER_SENT, REMINDER_SENT, ACCEPTED, DECLINED, EXPIRED, ESCALATED, REASSIGNED, UNASSIGNED, HOST_NOTIFIED, BOOKING_CHANGED, RECONFIRM_REQUESTED. You can trace exactly what happened and when.

Summary: What Oordio Can Do

Oordio automates the full lifecycle: booking → job → offer → accept → complete → pay. It syncs with your calendars, assigns cleaners in priority order, sends smart reminders, handles time changes, and processes payments. You get a unified dashboard and audit trail; cleaners get clear jobs and on-time payouts. The result: less manual coordination, fewer missed turnarounds, and more time for guest experience and growth.
