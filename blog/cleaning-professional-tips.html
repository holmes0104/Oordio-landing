5 Tips for Cleaning Professionals Working with Short-Term Rentals

Turnaround cleaning done right

Turnaround cleaning for Airbnb and VRBO is different from residential or commercial work. Tight windows, high standards, and fast payouts—here's how to thrive as a short-term rental cleaner.

1. Respond Quickly to Job Offers

Hosts often have a narrow window between checkout and the next guest. If you're offered a job, respond within the acceptance window. Delays mean the host moves to a backup, and you miss the work. Apps like Oordio send push notifications—enable them and check your phone regularly on days you're available.

The acceptance window varies by urgency: when checkout is days away, you might have 48 hours; when it's same-day, you might have 60 minutes. Oordio sends reminders at 50%, 75%, and 90% of the window so you don't miss an offer. Respond as soon as you know—it keeps you top of mind for future jobs.

2. Follow the Checklist

Every property has a checklist: linens, surfaces, amenities, photos. Stick to it. Hosts rely on consistency for guest reviews. Skipping steps or improvising can lead to complaints and lost repeat business.

Checklists are visible in the Oordio app when you accept a job. Check off each item as you complete it. If something is damaged or missing, note it—hosts appreciate the heads-up. A thorough checklist run builds trust and makes you the go-to cleaner for that property.

3. Document Your Work

Photo verification isn't just for the host—it protects you. If a guest claims something wasn't clean, your completion photos are proof. Upload them through the app as soon as you're done.

Focus on key areas: made beds, clean bathroom, stocked kitchen, overall tidiness. A few clear photos go a long way. Hosts get peace of mind; you get a record that the job was done to standard.

4. Get Paid Automatically

Chasing invoices is a waste of time. Work with hosts who use automated payouts—payment is captured when the job is approved and sent to you without follow-up. You focus on cleaning, not accounting.

Oordio integrates with Stripe so cleaners can connect their account and receive payouts automatically after job completion. No invoicing, no "I'll send it next week." Connect once, get paid on time every time.

5. Build Relationships

Hosts remember reliable cleaners. Show up on time, communicate clearly, and deliver consistent quality. Many hosts have a primary and backup—being the go-to primary means more steady work and fewer gaps.

If you're the primary cleaner for a property, you get first dibs on every turnaround. That's predictable income. If you're the backup, you get offers when the primary can't make it. Either way, reliability and consistency build lasting relationships.

Short-term rental cleaning can be lucrative and flexible. These tips help you work smarter and get paid on time.
