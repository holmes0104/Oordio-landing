Why Every Host Needs a Backup Cleaner

Protect your reviews and your sanity

Your primary cleaner is reliable. They know your property, your standards, and they've never let you down. Until they do—sick day, car trouble, or a scheduling conflict. Without a backup, you're left scrambling hours before checkout.

The Cost of "No Backup"

When your only cleaner can't make it, you're forced to either clean yourself (if you're nearby), pay premium rates for last-minute help, or risk a delayed check-in. Guests don't care about your logistics—they expect a clean place on time. One bad review can cost you more than a year of backup cleaner coordination.

Consider the math: a single 4-star review instead of 5 can drop your listing's visibility. A delayed check-in can trigger refunds, complaints, and lasting damage to your reputation. The cost of one missed turnaround often exceeds the effort of setting up a backup cleaner.

Building Your Backup Ladder

Think of it as a ladder: primary first, then backup, then marketplace. Your backup should be someone you've vetted—maybe a cleaner who's filled in before, a trusted referral from another host, or someone from a local cleaning network. They don't need to be your go-to for every job, but they should be ready when your primary isn't.

Ideally, your backup knows your property (or at least your standards). If that's not possible, give them access to your digital checklist so they know exactly what's expected. The goal is consistency: guests shouldn't notice a difference whether your primary or backup cleaned.

Automate the Handoff

Manual backup coordination is painful. You text primary, wait, then text backup, wait again. If you're juggling multiple properties, the back-and-forth multiplies. Tools like Oordio handle this automatically: if your primary declines or doesn't respond within the acceptance window, the job is offered to your backup with a push notification. No double-booking, no missed messages, no "I thought you were handling it."

Oordio's assignment ladder runs in order: primary → backup → marketplace → unassigned. You're notified only when all options are exhausted. Until then, the system handles the handoff.

When to Use the Marketplace

If both primary and backup are unavailable, Oordio can create a marketplace posting (on Pro and Business plans). Vetted cleaners browse and bid; you review and accept. It's a fallback, not a replacement for your core team—but it's there when you need it.

A backup cleaner isn't a luxury—it's insurance. Set one up before you need one.
